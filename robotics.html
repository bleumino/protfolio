Independent Learning / Robotics Engineering

Exploring Robotics Engineering.

After discovering the rapid growth of physical AI, I decided to independently study robotics engineering and build the foundations needed to enter the field.

My learning roadmap is based on Flinders University's 2025 Bachelor of Engineering (Robotics) (Honours) study plan. This page documents the resources I've used along the way.

Areas of study

Physics 1A · PHYS1101 Engineering Maths · ENGR2711 Electronics · ENGR1201 Signals and Systems · ENGR2722 Electronic Circuits · ENGR2731 Sensors and Actuators · ENGR2772 Robotic Systems · ENGR3771 Control Systems · ENGR3711

Course selection: These subjects are based on the 2025 Flinders University curriculum, the year I commenced my degree. I selected them to highlight the mathematics, physics, electronics, robotics and control systems knowledge relevant to robotics, presenting them as a focused, minor-like area of study within my broader degree.

ENGR2711

Calculus III

Krista King

A broad collection of Calculus III topics, organised into several areas:

Partial Derivatives — limits and continuity, partial derivatives, tangent planes, linear approximation, chain rule, directional derivatives, gradients, optimisation and Lagrange multipliers.

Multiple Integrals — double and triple integrals, polar coordinates, cylindrical and spherical coordinates, applications and change of variables.

Vectors — coordinate systems, dot and cross products, lines and planes, vector functions, arc length, curvature, line integrals, Green's theorem, curl, divergence and surface integrals.

Differential Equations — linear, separable and exact equations, population modelling, Euler's method, second-order equations and Laplace transforms.
