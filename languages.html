02 / The Process

My Language Journey

I enjoy approaching language learning as a long-term process rather than simply memorising vocabulary.

01

Listening

Getting familiar with natural speech, pronunciation, accents and everyday expressions through native content.

02

Understanding

Exploring grammar, sentence structures and vocabulary to understand how languages actually work.

03

Practising

Applying what I've learned through reading, writing, listening and communicating.

03 / A Personal Interest

Every language offers a different perspective.

Beyond the practical benefits, I find language learning interesting because it gives me an opportunity to explore different cultures and ways of expressing ideas.

I prefer learning independently, combining structured resources with real-world content such as videos, conversations, music and reading.

My goal isn't simply to collect languages, but to develop the ability to understand and communicate in them.
